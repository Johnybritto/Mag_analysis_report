# India Space Ecosystem Tracker — 17 August 2026

## Purpose

This tracker maps Indian space-sector companies from an investment perspective. Association with ISRO, Chandrayaan, Gaganyaan or a technology transfer is **not** sufficient for promotion into the investable shortlist.

The primary question is:

> Does every additional launch, satellite, constellation, ground-station customer or mission create another invoice, and can that repeat for years?

The tracker therefore separates **space reality**, **revenue recurrence**, **financial materiality**, **hiddenness/crowding**, and **proof level**.

### Revenue-model hierarchy

1. **Recurring service infrastructure — highest quality potential**: recurring ground-station usage, network/service contracts, AMC/O&M.
2. **Consumable / serial production — very good**: engines, rocket motors, alloys, fasteners and hardware that must be supplied again for every new launch/vehicle.
3. **Repeat subsystem manufacturing — good**: avionics, RF, electronics, optics, payload or satellite subsystems repeatedly supplied across missions.
4. **Technology association / one-off contribution — insufficient**: technology transfer or one historic mission contribution without evidence of repeat commercial revenue.

## 9. Mandatory Phase 3 Questions for Every Space Stock

1. How much rupee revenue comes from **space specifically** today?
2. Is that revenue separately disclosed, or are we inferring it from a broader Defence/Aerospace segment?
3. How frequently does the customer reorder?
4. Does each additional launch/satellite create another unit sale?
5. Is there AMC, O&M, consumable or subscription/service revenue after the initial sale?
6. How much comes from ISRO/NSIL versus private-space customers and exports?
7. Can private-space customers eventually become larger than ISRO for the company?
8. What is the current order book attributable to space?
9. What gross/EBITDA margin does the space business earn versus the rest of the company?
10. Does the company have enough capacity to scale without destroying cash flow?
11. Is space financially material enough to change consolidated EPS?
12. Is the market already pricing in the space opportunity?
13. What single KPI would disprove the space thesis?
