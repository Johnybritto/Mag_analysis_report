# Mandatory Water Theme Rules — Mag Analysis

**Status:** Mandatory for every future Mag Analysis

Every magazine analysis must include a separate section titled:

## Water Theme Correlation — Mandatory

This section must be present even when the magazine does not explicitly discuss water.

## Required scan

Check for direct or second-order implications for:

- water and wastewater treatment,
- desalination,
- sewage treatment,
- industrial water,
- water reuse and zero-liquid-discharge (ZLD),
- high-purity / ultrapure water,
- municipal water infrastructure,
- pumping and water transmission,
- membranes, resins and treatment chemicals,
- EPC and recurring O&M/service revenue,
- policy and public capex affecting water infrastructure.

## Mandatory correlation test

When an issue discusses any of the following themes, explicitly test whether it creates a water-demand or water-infrastructure read-through:

- semiconductors and electronics manufacturing,
- data centres / AI compute,
- power generation and thermal/renewable projects,
- chemicals and specialty chemicals,
- pharmaceuticals,
- steel/metals/mining,
- food and beverages,
- urbanisation and housing,
- industrial/manufacturing capex,
- airports, logistics parks and large infrastructure projects,
- climate, drought, monsoon or groundwater stress.

Do not force a link. If evidence is weak, say `No material water-theme correlation identified`.

## Mandatory names to assess

### VA Tech WABAG
Always check whether the magazine theme could affect:
- desalination,
- wastewater/reuse,
- municipal or industrial EPC,
- high-purity-water opportunities,
- O&M/recurring revenue,
- order intake and order quality,
- international/sovereign-funded projects,
- cash flow and receivables,
- valuation/entry setup when external verification is requested.

### Ion Exchange
Always check whether the theme could affect:
- industrial water,
- resins and treatment chemicals,
- high-purity/ultrapure water,
- Roha utilisation,
- chemicals margins,
- engineering margins,
- cash flow, receivables and debt,
- recurring products/services versus project revenue.

### Wider peer scan
When relevant, scan credible peers/beneficiaries such as EMS Ltd, Enviro Infra Engineers, Denta Water, SPML Infra/JWIL and other listed water/environmental infrastructure companies. Do not add names only for completeness.

## Evidence rule

Clearly label each finding as one of:

- **Magazine fact** — explicitly stated in the issue.
- **Analytical inference** — second-order correlation derived from the magazine theme.
- **External verification needed** — current orders, earnings, valuation, price, technical levels or company-specific data not supported by the issue itself.

Never convert an inferred water correlation into a magazine fact.

## Required output

The section must conclude with one of:

- **Positive / actionable correlation**
- **Watch / emerging correlation**
- **Negative / thesis risk**
- **No material water-theme correlation identified**

If the correlation is meaningful, state which company or sub-theme deserves Phase 3 or further external verification.
